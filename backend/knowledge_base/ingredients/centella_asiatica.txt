Ingredient: Centella Asiatica
INCI name: Centella Asiatica Extract, Centella Asiatica Leaf Extract, Centella Asiatica Leaf Water
Also known as: Cica, Tiger Grass, Gotu Kola, Asiatic Pennywort, Brahmi (in Ayurvedic medicine)

Active compounds:
Centella asiatica contains four main bioactive triterpenes:
1. Madecassoside: Anti-inflammatory, wound healing, fibroblast stimulation
2. Asiaticoside: Collagen synthesis promotion, wound healing
3. Asiatic acid: Anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, collagen synthesis
4. Madecassic acid: Anti-inflammatory, antioxidant

Also contains: Flavonoids (quercetin, kaempferol), phytosterols, tannins, amino acids, polyunsaturated fatty acids

Mechanism of action:
1. Fibroblast stimulation → upregulates collagen I and III synthesis → structural skin support
2. NF-κB pathway inhibition → reduces pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-1β, IL-6, TNF-α) → anti-inflammatory
3. Accelerates re-epithelialization → wound healing and post-procedure recovery
4. Antioxidant protection → reduces oxidative stress from UV and environmental exposure
5. Inhibits MMP (matrix metalloproteinase) activity → prevents collagen degradation
6. Anti-itch properties → reduces histamine-triggered itch in eczema

Clinical applications with evidence:
- Rosacea: Reduces papule count and erythema (madecassoside mechanism)
- Post-laser/procedure recovery: Accelerates barrier restoration
- Minor wound healing: FDA-approved for OTC wound care in some countries
- Eczema symptom reduction: Anti-itch and barrier-supporting properties
- Stretch marks: Some evidence for prevention with early application

Effective concentrations:
- As extract: 1-5% typically; some concentrated products 10-80%
- Beauty of Joseon Centella Serum: 80% whole-plant extract
- Skin standardized to madecassoside: Look for products specifying active triterpene content

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 1-3: HIGHEST VALUE for rosacea management. Madecassoside is one of the best-documented OTC ingredients for reducing rosacea-associated erythema and papules. Studies show significant symptom reduction comparable to low-dose azelaic acid. Preferred in the morning routine under SPF.
- Tones 4-7: Useful for general inflammation reduction, post-acne recovery, and general sensitive/reactive skin. The collagen-stimulating properties benefit all skin tones for long-term skin quality.
- Tones 7-10: Valuable for shortening the post-inflammatory phase after acne. Less inflammatory time = less PIH. Also supports barrier health in eczema-prone skin, reducing flares that cause PIH.
- Post-procedure (ALL tones): Centella is widely recommended by dermatologists for post-laser, post-peel, and post-extraction recovery. Accelerates the window of PIH risk.

Interactions:
- All common actives: Compatible. No known negative interactions.
- Niacinamide: Synergistic anti-inflammatory stacking
- Ceramides: Complementary — centella stimulates collagen synthesis, ceramides repair lipid barrier
- Retinol: Centella on off-retinol nights or together can reduce retinol irritation

Sensitization risk: Low. Contact dermatitis has been documented but is uncommon. Potential cross-reactivity with marigold/Asteraceae family plants.
Pregnancy safety: Safe
Photosensitivity: None
Available in: Serums, toners, sheet masks, moisturizers, essences
