Ingredient Category: Sunscreen / SPF Filters
Types: Chemical UV filters, Mineral UV filters (Zinc Oxide, Titanium Dioxide)

Why SPF is non-negotiable:
UV radiation is the primary driver of: photoaging (wrinkles, loss of elasticity), skin cancer, worsening of hyperpigmentation, melasma, and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation. SPF is the single most impactful skincare product available — more than any serum or treatment.

SPF rating explained:
- SPF 15: Filters 93% of UVB rays
- SPF 30: Filters 97% of UVB rays
- SPF 50: Filters 98% of UVB rays
- SPF 100: Filters 99% of UVB rays
- Minimum recommended by AAD: SPF 30 for daily use

UVA vs UVB:
- UVB: Burns (SPF measures only UVB protection)
- UVA: Ages and penetrates deeper (causes oxidative damage, photoaging, hyperpigmentation)
- Broad-spectrum SPF: Required — covers BOTH UVA and UVB

Chemical filters (transparent, no white cast):
- Avobenzone: UVA absorber (best UVA coverage among OTC chemicals)
- Homosalate: UVB absorber
- Octisalate: UVB absorber, also stabilizes avobenzone
- Octinoxate: UVB absorber (restricted in Hawaii due to coral reef concerns)
- Octocrylene: UVA + UVB, stabilizer for avobenzone
- Oxybenzone: Broad spectrum (controversial — FDA studying absorption; some skin/hormone sensitivity claims — not definitively proven harmful at topical levels, but some people prefer to avoid)

Mineral filters (white cast potential, no absorption concerns):
- Zinc Oxide: Broad spectrum (UVA + UVB), anti-inflammatory, sebum-regulating, safest for pregnancy
- Titanium Dioxide: Primarily UVB + short UVA, needs combination with ZnO for full broad-spectrum

Monk tone specific notes — CRITICAL:
- Tones 1-3: All sunscreen types work. Mineral SPFs (titanium dioxide, zinc oxide) leave minimal white cast on light skin tones and are preferred for rosacea (chemical filters can cause vasodilation/flushing) and pregnancy.
- Tones 4-5: Hybrid mineral/chemical SPFs (e.g., EltaMD UV Clear with 9% zinc oxide) work well — manageable white cast with thorough blending. Full mineral formulas are borderline for tone 5.
- Tones 6-8: Chemical-only SPFs required for cosmetically acceptable wear. Zinc oxide at >5% causes visible white/grey cast on these skin tones. Black Girl Sunscreen SPF 30 or Fenty Skin Hydra Vizor are formulated explicitly for this range.
- Tones 9-10: Chemical filters essential. Even 5% zinc oxide is visible on the deepest Monk tones. Any mineral filter creates grey/ash cast that is cosmetically problematic.
- SPF + hyperpigmentation: UV exposure stimulates melanin production and worsens ALL forms of hyperpigmentation (sun spots, PIH, melasma). SPF is the most important product in any PIH routine — without it, treatments are partially undone every morning.
- Post-retinol: SPF is mandatory after any retinoid use due to photosensitivity

Application notes:
- Apply last in AM routine (after moisturizer, before makeup)
- ¼ teaspoon for face alone
- Reapply every 2 hours in direct sun
- SPF in makeup is not sufficient alone — too little product applied to reach labeled SPF value

Sensitization risk: Chemical filters — oxybenzone can cause contact sensitization; others generally well-tolerated. Mineral filters — extremely low sensitization risk.
Pregnancy safety: Mineral filters (zinc oxide, titanium dioxide) preferred. Chemical filters — consult OB-GYN. Avobenzone and oxybenzone are Category C (FDA).
