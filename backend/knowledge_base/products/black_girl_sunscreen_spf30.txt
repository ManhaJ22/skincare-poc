Why it works:
One of the only sunscreens formulated with deep skin tones as the primary target consumer. Chemical UV filters (avobenzone, homosalate, octisalate) are transparent on skin — no white cast, no grey cast, no ashy residue. This is a fundamental chemical vs mineral distinction: zinc oxide and titanium dioxide (mineral filters) leave white residue that is visible and aesthetically unacceptable for most skin on the medium-to-deep end of the Monk scale. Sunflower oil and jojoba oil provide lightweight moisturization, and cacao butter adds a subtle warmth-enhancing tint that complements deeper skin tones. The result is a sunscreen that wears like a light moisturizer and disappears into the skin. SPF 30 filters 97% of UVB rays — clinically sufficient for daily use.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 6-10: This is the go-to daily SPF recommendation. The chemical filter system means zero white cast or ashiness. Deeply hydrating without being greasy. The subtle warmth from cacao butter slightly enhances skin tone rather than washing it out.
- Tones 4-5: Also appropriate — no white cast, comfortable wear.
- Tones 1-3: Chemical filters are safe and effective across all tones, though those with melasma or photosensitive conditions should consider SPF 50+. The moisturizing oils may be heavy for very oily lighter skin tones.
- ALL tones: UV exposure contributes to PIH in all skin tones. SPF is arguably the most important product in any hyperpigmentation-focused routine.

Not recommended for:
- Monk tones 1-3 with oily skin who want a lighter SPF
- Users with oxybenzone sensitivity (does not contain oxybenzone — but confirm chemical filter tolerance)
- Those seeking SPF 50+ protection (this is SPF 30)
- Users preferring mineral-only sunscreens

Dermatologist note: Chemical sunscreens are FDA-approved and have decades of safety data. Concerns about avobenzone being carcinogenic are not supported by current evidence at topical application levels. SPF 30 is the AAD minimum recommendation for daily use. Safe during pregnancy — but for highest safety during pregnancy, some prefer mineral-only SPF.
