Why it works:
Colloidal oatmeal at 1% is an FDA-recognized skin protectant (monograph active ingredient) with clinical evidence for reducing eczema itch, inflammation, and skin barrier disruption. Oatmeal contains avenanthramides, a class of polyphenols with anti-itch, anti-inflammatory, and skin barrier-supportive properties. The 1% concentration is the minimum effective FDA monograph dose. Shea butter provides lipid-rich occlusion, ceramides support barrier restoration, and allantoin softens and soothes. White willow bark provides very mild BHA activity (natural salicin) that reduces flakiness without the irritation of synthetic salicylic acid. Together: immediate soothing + barrier repair + gentle exfoliation of dead skin that accumulates on eczema-prone skin.

Monk tone specific notes:
- ALL tones: Eczema and dry/very dry skin are not tone-specific concerns. This cream is appropriate as a therapeutic moisturizer for any Monk tone experiencing a compromised skin barrier.
- Tones 7-10: In eczema-prone deeper skin tones, the post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation from eczema flares can be significant. Using an effective eczema moisturizer to reduce flare frequency directly reduces PIH accumulation.

Not recommended for:
- Oily skin (very rich and occlusive — suitable for dry body areas but may cause milia on oily facial skin)
- Users wanting a lightweight moisturizer
- Oat allergy (cross-reactive in some individuals — patch test required)

Dermatologist note: Colloidal oatmeal 1% is an FDA-approved active ingredient for skin protection. Recommended by the National Eczema Association. Safe during pregnancy and breastfeeding. Safe for infants. Particularly effective as a body moisturizer for eczema-prone skin.
