Why it works:
Vanicream is formulated by a pharmacist-founded company with the explicit goal of eliminating every known common skin irritant: no dyes, no fragrance (including "fragrance-free" loopholes like masking fragrances), no lanolin, no parabens, no formaldehyde releasers, no MI/MCI (methylisothiazolinone). Gluconolactone is a polyhydroxy acid (PHA) — a large-molecule cousin of AHAs — that provides very gentle exfoliation without penetrating to deeper skin layers, making it safe even for compromised skin. Sodium cocoyl isethionate is one of the mildest surfactants available (gentler than sodium lauryl/laureth sulfate by significant margin). The result: a cleanser appropriate for patch test-level sensitivity, eczema flare states, and the most reactive skin types.

Monk tone specific notes:
- ALL tones: This is the first-line recommendation for any user reporting sensitivity, eczema, rosacea, or fragrance reactions. The complete allergen exclusion profile makes it safe for the broadest possible user base.
- Tones 7-10: For users with PIH who are washing their face with irritating cleansers (even unintentionally), switching to Vanicream can reduce background inflammation and allow PIH to heal faster. Low-grade irritation from harsh cleansers is an underappreciated cause of ongoing PIH.

Not recommended for:
- Users who specifically want a medicated cleanser (salicylic acid, benzoyl peroxide) — this is a pure, gentle cleanser only
- Those who prefer a rich lather (very gentle foam profile)

Dermatologist note: Recommended by the National Eczema Association. Safe during pregnancy and breastfeeding. Safe for pediatric use including infants. The gold standard for elimination diets when trying to identify skin allergens — if a user is reacting to everything, starting with Vanicream creates a clean baseline.
