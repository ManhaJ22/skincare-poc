PRODUCT: CeraVe Hydrating Facial Cleanser (16 fl oz)
BRAND: CeraVe
AMAZON ASIN: B01MSSDEPK
PRICE RANGE: $12-$18 (16 fl oz bottle, lasts 4-6 months with twice-daily use)
ROUTINE STEP: cleanser
TIME OF USE: both (morning and evening)

KEY INGREDIENTS:
- Ceramides 1, 3, 6-II: lipids identical to those in the skin's own barrier. They refill the "mortar" between skin cells, reducing water loss after washing.
- Hyaluronic acid: humectant that binds water to the skin surface, so skin does not feel tight after rinsing.
- Glycerin: humectant and barrier-support ingredient; one of the most studied moisturizing agents.
- Phytosphingosine: a ceramide precursor with mild soothing properties.

SKIN TYPES: dry, normal, sensitive, combination (dry areas). Fine for mild-to-moderate acne-prone skin that is also dehydrated, or when using drying treatments like benzoyl peroxide or retinoids.

MONK SKIN TONES: works for all tones 1-10. Notes for deeper tones (Monk 6-10): a non-stripping cleanser lowers the risk of irritation-driven post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH), which is more common and longer-lasting on deeper skin. It also helps prevent the ashy, dull look dehydrated skin can have on deeper tones.

FRAGRANCE FREE: yes
CRUELTY FREE: not certified; check the brand's current status if this matters to the user.
NON-COMEDOGENIC: yes

WHY IT WORKS: Harsh surfactants strip barrier lipids, which raises transepidermal water loss (TEWL). This lotion-texture, non-foaming formula uses mild cleansing agents and puts ceramides and humectants back during the wash. The barrier stays intact, and an intact barrier tolerates actives (retinoids, acids, vitamin C) better.

NOT GOOD FOR:
- Removing heavy waterproof makeup or mineral sunscreen alone. Double cleanse with an oil or balm first.
- Very oily skin that wants a squeaky-clean feel. It can feel like it "didn't clean" on very oily skin.
- It is not an acne treatment; it contains no salicylic acid or benzoyl peroxide.
