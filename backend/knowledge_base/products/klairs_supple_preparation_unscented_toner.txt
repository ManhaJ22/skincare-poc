Why it works:
A hydrating (not exfoliating) toner. Hyaluronic acid and glycerin are humectants that pull water into the outer layer of skin right after cleansing, when skin is most prone to tightness. Beta-glucan and centella calm the skin, and panthenol supports barrier repair. Damp, hydrated skin also spreads the next layers (serums, moisturizer) more evenly. It contains no alcohol, acids, essential oils, or fragrance, which is why it suits reactive skin.

Monk tone specific notes:
- ALL tones: Hydration is not tone-specific. Well-hydrated skin looks more even and less dull at every Monk tone.
- Tones 6-10: Dehydrated deeper skin can look ashy or grey; a hydrating toner layer helps prevent that without adding shine. Its calming ingredients also lower the irritation that can trigger dark marks.

Not recommended for:
- Users wanting exfoliation or oil control from their toner (this is purely hydrating)
- Very tight budgets (an optional step)
- Anyone needing a minimal 3-step routine
