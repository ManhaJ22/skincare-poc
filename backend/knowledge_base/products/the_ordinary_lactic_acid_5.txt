Why it works:
Lactic acid is an alpha hydroxy acid (AHA) that loosens the bonds between dead surface skin cells so they shed more evenly, which smooths texture and brightens dullness. Its larger molecule penetrates more slowly than glycolic acid, making it one of the gentlest AHAs, and it is also a natural humectant. At 5% it is a beginner strength. The hyaluronic acid crosspolymer adds hydration, and Tasmanian pepperberry is included to reduce the sting acids can cause.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 1-6: A gentle way to smooth texture and fade light marks.
- Tones 7-10: Over-exfoliation is a common cause of new dark patches on deeper skin. Start once or twice a week, stop if it stings or peels, and never stack with other acids or retinoids on the same night.
- ALL tones: AHAs increase sun sensitivity; daily SPF is required.

Not recommended for:
- Very sensitive or compromised skin
- Using alongside retinol, adapalene, BHA, or benzoyl peroxide on the same night
- Daily use by beginners
- Deeper tones (Monk 7-10) prone to dark marks, without a slow start
