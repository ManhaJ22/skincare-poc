Why it works:
EltaMD UV Clear is a hybrid sunscreen (partial mineral, partial chemical) that has become a dermatologist favorite because it addresses two problems simultaneously: UV protection AND acne/sensitivity. Zinc oxide at 9% provides broad-spectrum UVA coverage with mild anti-inflammatory and sebum-regulating properties. Octinoxate handles UVB. Together they reach SPF 46. The addition of niacinamide provides ongoing pore-minimizing and anti-inflammatory benefit post-application. Hyaluronic acid maintains moisture balance under the filter layer. The formula is lightly tinted in the standard version, which helps reduce the visible white cast from zinc oxide.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 1-4: Ideal — the hybrid filter means less white cast than pure mineral, plus the niacinamide benefit for skin health throughout the day.
- Tones 5-7: The 9% zinc oxide creates mild white cast that is manageable with thorough blending or when worn under tinted moisturizer or foundation. Acceptable daily option.
- Tones 8-10: White cast becomes cosmetically problematic. Recommend Black Girl Sunscreen SPF 30 or a high-SPF chemical-filter sunscreen instead.
- Acne-prone skin across ALL tones: The combination of zinc oxide + niacinamide in a sunscreen is unique and particularly valuable — most SPF products worsen acne, this one actively helps.

Not recommended for:
- Monk tones 8-10 (visible white cast)
- Users who want 100% mineral formulation
- Budget-conscious users ($39 is the highest price point in this knowledge base)

Dermatologist note: Recommended by the American Academy of Dermatology for acne-prone skin. Non-comedogenic. Safe during pregnancy — hybrid filters are generally considered acceptable with physician guidance. The tinted version is preferred by many users for reduced white cast.
