Why it works:
The skin around the eyes is thinner and has fewer oil glands than the rest of the face, so it dehydrates faster and shows dryness lines first. Ceramides reinforce that thin barrier, hyaluronic acid adds hydration that temporarily plumps fine lines, and niacinamide supports barrier function and evens tone. The formula is fragrance-free and gentle enough for the eye area.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 1-4: Dark circles on lighter skin are often from visible blood vessels (bluish/purple); hydration can make them look a little less prominent.
- Tones 5-10: Under-eye darkness on deeper skin is commonly pigment-based (periorbital hyperpigmentation). This cream hydrates and supports the area, but it will not fade pigment-based circles much on its own. Daily sunscreen around the eyes helps prevent them from deepening.

Not recommended for:
- Expecting it to erase dark circles or puffiness (results are modest)
- Very tight budgets (an optional step; a gentle face moisturizer can work around the eyes)
- Treating pigment-based dark circles alone
