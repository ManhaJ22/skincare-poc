Why it works:
Fenty Skin was specifically launched by Rihanna with deeper skin tones as the primary target demographic — a departure from the historical assumption that beauty products are designed for tones 1-4 with deeper tones as afterthought. The Hydra Vizor combines moisturizer and SPF 30 in one step using chemical filters (no white cast) alongside niacinamide (10%) for ongoing pore and PIH management, and hyaluronic acid for hydration. The formula has a dewy, luminous finish that works with the natural radiance of medium-to-deep skin tones rather than muting them with a matte or white cast. Designed to not oxidize or turn ashy over time on deeper skin tones — a common failure mode of SPF moisturizers not designed with deeper tones in mind.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 6-10: This is a flagship recommendation for simplifying a morning routine. SPF + niacinamide + hydration in one step, designed to look beautiful on deep skin tones. The luminous finish complements rather than dulls.
- Tones 4-5: Also excellent — similar finish to Black Girl Sunscreen but with the added niacinamide benefit.
- Tones 1-3: Chemical filters work, but the dewy finish may feel too shiny for lighter oily skin types. Better options exist (EltaMD UV Clear for acne-prone, La Roche-Posay for sensitive).

Not recommended for:
- Oily skin (dewy finish may become too glossy)
- Those wanting SPF 50+
- Monk tones 1-3 with oily skin

Dermatologist note: SPF 30 is the AAD minimum for daily protection. The niacinamide content is meaningful at 10% (confirmed by brand). Chemical filter system is safe. No photosensitizing ingredients. Safe during pregnancy (chemical filter caveat — consult OB-GYN if desired).
