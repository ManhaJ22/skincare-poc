PRODUCT: The Ordinary Niacinamide 10% + Zinc 1% (30 mL)
BRAND: The Ordinary (DECIEM)
AMAZON ASIN: B01MDTVZTZ
PRICE RANGE: $6-$12 (30 mL; about 2-3 months using a few drops once or twice daily)
ROUTINE STEP: serum
TIME OF USE: both (morning and/or evening, after cleansing, before moisturizer)

KEY INGREDIENTS:
- Niacinamide (vitamin B3) 10%: lowers sebum production, strengthens the barrier by increasing ceramide synthesis, and reduces the transfer of pigment (melanosomes) from melanocytes to skin cells. That fades dark spots and post-acne marks.
- Zinc PCA 1%: zinc salt that helps regulate oil and has mild anti-inflammatory effects on blemish-prone skin.

SKIN TYPES: oily, combination, acne-prone, normal. Usually tolerated by sensitive skin, but 10% is a high concentration and causes redness or itching in some users.

MONK SKIN TONES: works for all tones 1-10. Notes for deeper tones (Monk 6-10): niacinamide is one of the safest brightening ingredients for deeper skin. It fades PIH without bleaching normal skin and has a low irritation risk compared with hydroquinone or strong acids. If it irritates, irritation itself can cause new dark marks on deeper skin, so cut back to every other day.

FRAGRANCE FREE: yes
CRUELTY FREE: yes (vegan)
NON-COMEDOGENIC: yes; water-based serum

WHY IT WORKS: Niacinamide is studied at 2-5% for pigmentation and barrier repair and at up to 10% for oil control. Zinc complements its sebum-regulating effect. Because it is not an acid and does not change skin pH, it layers with most routines.

NOT GOOD FOR:
- Very sensitive or reactive skin (some people flush or itch at 10%). A 4-5% niacinamide product suits them better.
- Dry skin as a standalone. It is not moisturizing on its own.
- Layering under some silicone-heavy products. It can pill.
- Deep pigmentation such as melasma alone. Results are gradual (8-12 weeks) and need daily SPF.
