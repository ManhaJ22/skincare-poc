PRODUCT: La Roche-Posay Anthelios Tinted Mineral Sunscreen for Face SPF 50 (Ultra-Light Fluid)
BRAND: La Roche-Posay
AMAZON ASIN: B007U54P36
PRICE RANGE: $33-$40 (1.7 fl oz / 50 mL; about 1-2 months of daily face use at the correct amount)
ROUTINE STEP: spf
TIME OF USE: morning (reapply every 2 hours in direct sun)

KEY INGREDIENTS:
- Titanium dioxide: mineral (physical) UV filter. Strong UVB protection and partial UVA protection; it reflects and absorbs UV.
- Zinc oxide: mineral UV filter covering UVB and the full UVA range (including long UVA1), which drives pigmentation and photoaging.
- Iron oxides (tint): pigments that also block part of visible light. Visible light worsens melasma and hyperpigmentation, especially in deeper skin tones.
- Perlite / silica: oil-absorbing minerals that give a matte finish.

SKIN TYPES: sensitive, normal, combination, oily (matte finish). Suitable after procedures or for reactive skin, because mineral filters are less likely to sting than chemical filters.

MONK SKIN TONES: best match for Monk 1-5. Notes for deeper tones (Monk 6-10): the tint is light-to-medium, so it can still leave a grey/ashy cast on deep skin, even though the tint reduces the classic mineral white cast. For Monk 7-10, test on the jawline in daylight first. If it looks ashy, a sheer chemical-filter sunscreen is a better match. Deeper tones still need daily SPF: UV and visible light drive hyperpigmentation and melasma regardless of tone.

FRAGRANCE FREE: yes
CRUELTY FREE: not certified; check the brand's current status if this matters to the user.
OIL FREE / NON-COMEDOGENIC: yes
WATER RESISTANT: yes (40 minutes)

WHY IT WORKS: Zinc oxide gives broad, photostable UVA coverage, and the iron-oxide tint adds visible-light protection that untinted sunscreens lack. That makes it especially useful for hyperpigmentation, melasma, and post-acne marks. Mineral filters sit on the skin and are rarely sensitizing.

NOT GOOD FOR:
- Deep skin tones (Monk 8-10) that dislike any tint mismatch. The cast can look grey.
- Very dry skin. The matte, oil-absorbing finish can feel tight.
- Budgets under ~$20/month for skincare overall. It is the priciest product in this set.
- Heavy sweating or swimming beyond 40 minutes without reapplying.
