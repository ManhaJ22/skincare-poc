Why it works:
Hydrocolloid is a wound-care material that absorbs fluid from a surfaced pimple and keeps the area moist and protected, which helps it flatten and heal faster. It contains no active drug, so it adds no dryness or irritation to the rest of a routine. Just as important, the patch is a physical barrier: it stops picking and squeezing, which is one of the biggest causes of acne scars and long-lasting dark marks.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 5-10: Especially valuable. Picking and inflamed breakouts are what leave post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) on deeper skin, often for months. Preventing that trauma is one of the most effective ways to avoid new dark marks.
- ALL tones: Gentle enough to use alongside any routine, including retinoids or acids.

Not recommended for:
- Deep, under-the-skin cysts or nodules that have no head (little to absorb)
- Treating widespread acne on its own (it only covers individual spots)
- Preventing breakouts (it treats existing spots)
