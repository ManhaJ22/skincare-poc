Why it works:
Cetaphil Gentle Skin Cleanser is one of the most dermatologist-recommended cleansers globally and has decades of clinical use supporting its safety and efficacy. It is a non-foaming, rinse-off or wipe-off formula that removes surface impurities and makeup without disrupting the skin's natural moisture barrier. The reformulated version contains niacinamide and panthenol, adding active barrier-supporting benefit to what was previously just a gentle cleanser. Glycerin is the primary humectant, preventing moisture loss during cleansing. The formula uses no sulfates, no fragrance, and minimal actives — making it appropriate as the "cleanest" possible cleanser baseline for anyone starting out or with very reactive skin.

Monk tone specific notes:
- ALL tones: Truly universal. Recommended as the cleanser of choice when someone is unsure what their skin is doing or wants to simplify. The lack of actives means it won't interfere with any other product in the routine.
- Tones 7-10: The absence of any potential irritants makes this useful for users with frequent PIH — eliminating cleanser-driven irritation is an important first step.

Not recommended for:
- Users with significant active acne who need a medicated cleanser
- Makeup-heavy users (may need a dedicated oil cleanser or micellar water first for makeup removal, followed by Cetaphil as second cleanse)
- Those who prefer a lathering foam experience

Dermatologist note: Safe during pregnancy. Endorsed by the American Academy of Dermatology. Safe for pediatric and infant use. The simplest possible starting point for any skincare routine.
