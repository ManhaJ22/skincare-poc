Why it works:
CeraVe has engineered a gentler retinol delivery through encapsulation technology — the retinol is enclosed in microspheres that release slowly over time, dramatically reducing the initial retinization (dryness, peeling, redness) that causes many users to abandon retinol. What makes this formula distinctive is the pairing of retinol with: niacinamide (anti-inflammatory, simultaneously supports barrier and reduces PIH) and licorice root extract (glabridin, a tyrosinase inhibitor with brightening properties). Ceramides maintain barrier integrity throughout the retinization process. This combination uniquely addresses a major problem for Monk tones 5-10: retinol's irritation potential can cause PIH, but the built-in niacinamide actively works to prevent PIH while the retinol works. This is a thoughtfully formulated retinol for diverse skin tones.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 4-8: One of the best-tolerated retinol serums for medium skin tones. The encapsulated delivery + niacinamide makes the PIH risk significantly lower than standard retinol serums. Start 2x per week.
- Tones 1-3: Excellent gentle retinol. Licorice root extract adds brightening benefit on top of retinol's cell turnover benefit.
- Tones 9-10: Still requires caution. Use the sandwich technique (moisturizer → wait → retinol → wait → moisturizer). Begin 1x per week. Encapsulation helps but does not eliminate PIH risk entirely for the deepest skin tones.
- ALL tones: Mandatory SPF the next morning.

Not recommended for:
- Pregnant or breastfeeding users (retinoids are contraindicated)
- Monk tones 9-10 without the sandwich technique
- Combination with AHAs, BHAs the same evening

Dermatologist note: Do not use during pregnancy. The licorice root extract (glabridin) is a meaningful addition for brightening. Encapsulated retinol is well-documented in the dermatology literature for reducing side effects vs. conventional delivery. Safe with consistent SPF use.
