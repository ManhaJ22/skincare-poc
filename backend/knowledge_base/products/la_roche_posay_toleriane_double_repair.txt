Why it works:
Ceramide-3 replaces lipids missing from a damaged barrier, cutting the water loss that makes skin tight and reactive. Niacinamide boosts the skin's own ceramide production and calms visible redness. Glycerin draws water into the outer skin layer. The formula is deliberately minimal (no fragrance, no essential oils, oil-free), which is why it is a common recommendation for rosacea-prone and post-procedure skin and for buffering retinoids.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 1-5: Especially useful because redness and flushing are most visible on lighter tones, and this formula is built around calming them.
- Tones 6-10: A solid barrier moisturizer that leaves no cast or residue. Keeping the barrier intact lowers the risk of irritation-driven PIH when using actives.
- ALL tones: Good partner for retinoids, benzoyl peroxide, or acids.

Not recommended for:
- Very dry skin that needs a rich, occlusive cream (this is lightweight)
- Users wanting a moisturizer with SPF built in
- Tight budgets (~$25)
