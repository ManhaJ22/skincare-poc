Why it works:
Tranexamic acid interferes with the plasmin pathway that UV and inflammation use to signal melanocytes to make more pigment, so it targets the trigger behind PIH and melasma rather than exfoliating the pigment away. Niacinamide works at a different step, reducing the transfer of finished melanin from melanocytes to surface skin cells. The two mechanisms are complementary, and neither is an acid, so the serum is low-irritation and fits most routines. Expect gradual change over 8-12 weeks.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 4-10: A cornerstone PIH product. Because it fades pigment without irritating the skin, it avoids the irritation-then-darkening cycle that harsher brighteners can cause on medium and deep skin. Pairs well with alpha arbutin or azelaic acid for a multi-pathway approach.
- Tones 1-3: Works for sun spots and post-acne marks, though lighter-tone post-acne marks are often red (post-inflammatory erythema) rather than brown, which this serum does not target.
- ALL tones: Results disappear without daily sunscreen.

Not recommended for:
- Red post-acne marks (erythema) as the main concern
- Active acne as a standalone treatment
- Anyone expecting fast results (8-12 weeks minimum)
