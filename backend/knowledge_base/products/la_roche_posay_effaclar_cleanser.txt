Why it works:
A gel-based foaming cleanser specifically formulated for oily, acne-prone skin without over-stripping. Zinc pidolate regulates sebum production and has mild antibacterial properties. Niacinamide at low concentration soothes post-breakout redness. La Roche-Posay's thermal spring water contains selenium, an antioxidant mineral that supports skin recovery. At pH 5.5, the formula preserves the acid mantle while effectively removing excess oil. The surfactant system (sodium laureth sulfate at carefully controlled levels) provides satisfying foam without the harshness that can trigger rebound oiliness. Soap-free and oil-free.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 4-8: Particularly relevant because excess oil production is a common trigger for PIH in medium and olive skin tones. Managing sebum reduces acne frequency and therefore reduces dark mark occurrence.
- Tones 7-10: Caution — use once daily only (morning) if skin feels tight after evening use. Combine with a rich ceramide moisturizer to maintain barrier integrity.
- Tones 1-3: Fine for oily skin regardless of tone. Those with rosacea should monitor for increased flushing — the foaming action can transiently increase skin temperature.

Not recommended for:
- Dry or sensitive skin (will strip moisture)
- Eczema-prone skin
- Users seeking a non-foaming formula

Dermatologist note: Hypoallergenic, non-comedogenic, tested on sensitive skin. Safe during pregnancy. No retinoids or salicylic acid in this formula.
