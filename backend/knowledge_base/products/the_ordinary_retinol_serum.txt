Why it works:
Retinol is the gold standard OTC vitamin A derivative. It converts on skin to retinoic acid (the active form), which binds to nuclear retinoid receptors, triggering: cell turnover acceleration, collagen synthesis stimulation, melanin production regulation, and comedolytic (pore-clearing) action. 0.2% is the lowest effective retinol concentration — ideal for beginners because it allows skin to adapt to retinization (initial dryness, flaking, and sensitivity) before moving to higher concentrations (0.5%, 1%). Squalane as the carrier is a skin-identical emollient that cushions the delivery, reducing the "purging" phase and making the formula gentler than water-based retinol serums.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 1-5: Excellent introduction to retinoids. Begin 1x per week, increase to 2x, then 3x over 8-12 weeks. Expect a purging period (2-4 weeks of slightly increased breakouts or flaking) — this is normal and temporary.
- Tones 6-10: Retinol requires careful management. Retinization irritation can cause PIH in deeper skin tones. The "sandwich technique" is recommended: apply moisturizer, wait 5 minutes, apply retinol 0.2%, wait 5 minutes, apply another layer of moisturizer. This buffers delivery and dramatically reduces irritation. Begin strictly 1x per week and do not increase frequency until there is zero redness or irritation for 4 consecutive weeks. Consider The Ordinary's Granactive Retinoid 2% as an even gentler alternative.
- ALL tones: MANDATORY sun protection the next morning. Retinol makes skin photosensitive.

Not recommended for:
- Pregnant or breastfeeding users (retinoids are contraindicated — use azelaic acid instead)
- Active eczema flare-ups
- Broken or sunburned skin
- Same evening as AHAs, BHAs, or vitamin C (can cause irritation)
- Monk tones 7-10 without the sandwich technique

Dermatologist note: Do not use during pregnancy or breastfeeding — this is an absolute contraindication. Start at 0.2%, not higher. Allow full retinization before increasing to 0.5%. Consistent SPF50 use is mandatory throughout retinol use.
