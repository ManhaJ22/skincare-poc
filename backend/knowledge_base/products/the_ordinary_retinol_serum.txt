Why it works:
Retinol is the gold standard OTC vitamin A derivative. It converts on skin to retinoic acid (the active form), which binds to nuclear retinoid receptors, triggering: cell turnover acceleration, collagen synthesis stimulation, melanin production regulation, and comedolytic (pore-clearing) action. 0.5% is a moderate, intermediate strength: The Ordinary positions it for people who have already built tolerance to a lower-strength retinol. True beginners can still use it, but should start less often and buffer it with moisturizer. Squalane as the carrier is a skin-identical emollient that cushions the delivery and offsets some of the dryness retinol causes, making the formula gentler than water-based retinol serums at the same strength.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 1-5: A solid step into retinoids. First-timers should begin 1x per week, increase to 2x, then 3x over 8-12 weeks. Expect a purging period (2-4 weeks of slightly increased breakouts or flaking); this is normal and temporary.
- Tones 6-8: Retinol requires careful management. Retinization irritation can cause PIH in deeper skin tones, and 0.5% is not an entry-level strength. Use the "sandwich technique": apply moisturizer, wait 5 minutes, apply the retinol, wait 5 minutes, apply another layer of moisturizer. Begin strictly 1x per week and do not increase frequency until there is zero redness or irritation for 4 consecutive weeks. A gentler option (CeraVe Resurfacing Retinol with built-in niacinamide) may suit first-timers better.
- ALL tones: MANDATORY sun protection the next morning. Retinol makes skin photosensitive.

Not recommended for:
- Pregnant or breastfeeding users (retinoids are contraindicated; use azelaic acid instead)
- Complete retinoid beginners with sensitive skin (start with a lower strength or an encapsulated retinol)
- Active eczema flare-ups
- Broken or sunburned skin
- Same evening as AHAs, BHAs, or vitamin C (can cause irritation)
- Monk tones 7-10 without the sandwich technique

Dermatologist note: Do not use during pregnancy or breastfeeding; this is an absolute contraindication. Do not combine with other retinoid products. Refrigerate after opening. Consistent SPF use is mandatory throughout retinol use.
