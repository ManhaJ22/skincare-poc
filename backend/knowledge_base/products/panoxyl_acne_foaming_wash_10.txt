Why it works:
Benzoyl peroxide releases oxygen inside the pore, which kills C. acnes bacteria directly. Bacteria cannot become resistant to it the way they can to antibiotics. It also has a mild peeling effect that helps clear clogged pores. As a wash, it is rinsed off after short contact, which is gentler than a leave-on BP. Even so, 10% is the strongest over-the-counter concentration and causes dryness, flaking, and stinging in many users.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 1-4: Effective for inflammatory acne. Expect some dryness; follow with a barrier moisturizer.
- Tones 5-10: Works on the acne, but irritation and dryness from 10% BP can trigger post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) that outlasts the breakout. Start 2-3 times per week with a 1-minute contact time, or choose a 4-5% BP or a gentler acne cleanser.
- ALL tones: Benzoyl peroxide bleaches fabric. Use white towels and pillowcases.

Not recommended for:
- Dry, sensitive, or eczema-prone skin
- Deeper tones (Monk 7-10) who are prone to dark marks, unless used very sparingly
- Using in the same routine as tretinoin or retinol without spacing them out (more irritation)
- Non-inflammatory concerns like hyperpigmentation alone
