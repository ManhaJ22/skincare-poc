Why it works:
A clear, silicone-based gel that uses only chemical UV filters, so there are no particles to scatter light and nothing to show on the skin at any tone. Avobenzone covers UVA; homosalate, octisalate, and octocrylene cover UVB and help stabilize avobenzone. Isododecane and dimethicone evaporate or set into a velvety, slightly mattifying film, which controls shine on oily skin and gives makeup something to grip.

Monk tone specific notes:
- ALL tones: Fully transparent, no white or grey cast, which makes it one of the few premium sunscreens that works equally well from Monk 1 to Monk 10.
- Tones 6-10: A good pick when a user wants an invisible matte finish rather than the dewy, moisturizing finish of Black Girl Sunscreen.
- Tones 1-3 with very reactive skin: chemical filters can sting some sensitive users; a mineral option may be gentler.

Not recommended for:
- Tight budgets (one of the most expensive sunscreens in the knowledge base at ~$38)
- Very dry skin that wants moisture from its sunscreen (no hydrating finish)
- Users who prefer mineral-only sunscreens
- People sensitive to silicones or to chemical UV filters
