Why it works:
Hyaluronic acid (HA) is a glycosaminoglycan that holds up to 1,000 times its weight in water. The INKEY List formula contains two molecular weights: high molecular weight HA forms a film on the skin surface that prevents water loss (humectant + occlusive effect), while low molecular weight HA penetrates the outer layers of the stratum corneum for deeper hydration. Matrixyl 3000 is a peptide combination (palmitoyl tripeptide-1 and palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7) with clinical evidence for collagen stimulation and wrinkle reduction with consistent 4+ week use. Together, the formula provides both immediate plumping from hydration and longer-term structural support.

Application technique matters: apply to slightly damp skin immediately after cleansing (or splash face lightly with water first). HA draws moisture from its environment — on dry skin in low-humidity conditions, it can actually pull moisture from deeper skin layers, causing transient tightness. Damp application ensures it draws from water on the surface instead.

Monk tone specific notes:
- ALL tones: Universal — this addresses dehydration, not a pigmentation or skin-type concern. Well-hydrated skin across all Monk tones heals faster, looks plumper, and has a more even appearance regardless of tone.
- Tones 7-10: Dehydration can exaggerate the appearance of ashy or dull skin in deeper tones. Consistent HA serum use significantly improves luminosity.

Not recommended for:
- Use on completely dry skin in very low-humidity environments (can cause temporary tightness — always apply to damp skin)
- Users allergic to any HA product (rare; HA is naturally occurring in skin)

Dermatologist note: Completely safe during pregnancy. HA is naturally produced by the body (present in joints, eyes, and skin). Topical HA does not affect systemic HA levels. No photosensitivity. Can be used AM and PM indefinitely.
