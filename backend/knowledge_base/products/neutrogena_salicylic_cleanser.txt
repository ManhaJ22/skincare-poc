Why it works:
Salicylic acid at 2% (the FDA maximum OTC limit) is a beta-hydroxy acid (BHA) that is oil-soluble, allowing it to penetrate into pores and dissolve the sebum and dead skin cells that cause comedonal acne. Aloe vera and chamomile provide some soothing action to counterbalance the exfoliating effect. This is a leave-on-style active delivered in a rinse-off format, making it gentler than a targeted salicylic acid toner. Effective for blackheads, whiteheads, and mild inflammatory acne.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 1-5: Well-tolerated in clinical use. Effective for acne without excessive irritation at the rinse-off concentration.
- Tones 6-10: Use with significant caution. Salicylic acid at 2% can cause post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) flares if skin becomes irritated. Start with 2-3x per week only. If any irritation occurs, discontinue and choose a gentler BHA at 0.5-1% in leave-on format instead (allows for better dose control). Combination with niacinamide moisturizer strongly recommended.
- ALL tones: Contains fragrance — avoid if fragrance sensitive.

Not recommended for:
- Dry, sensitive, or eczema-prone skin
- Users with fragrance allergies (contains fragrance)
- Daily use on Monk tones 6-10 (PIH risk)
- Combination with other exfoliants (retinol, AHAs) same evening

Dermatologist note: Not recommended during pregnancy (salicylic acid at high concentrations is cautioned, though rinse-off at 2% is generally considered low risk — advise consulting OB-GYN). Patch test recommended for tones 6-10.
