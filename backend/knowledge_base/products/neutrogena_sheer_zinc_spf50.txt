Why it works:
Zinc oxide is a broad-spectrum mineral filter that covers UVB and the full UVA range, including long UVA1, on its own. It sits on the skin surface and is one of the least sensitizing UV filters available, which is why it is the default for rosacea-prone, post-procedure, and reactive skin. At 21.6% it delivers SPF 50 without any chemical filters. The trade-off is that micronized zinc oxide still scatters visible light, so it leaves a white film that is barely noticeable on very light skin but obvious on medium and deep skin.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 1-3: Excellent daily SPF. The cast blends into lighter skin within a few minutes, and the mineral-only formula suits the redness and reactivity more visible on lighter tones.
- Tone 4: May look slightly chalky; test along the jawline in daylight.
- Tones 5-10: Not recommended. The white/grey cast is clearly visible and commonly leads people to skip sunscreen altogether. A sheer chemical-filter sunscreen (e.g. Black Girl Sunscreen, Supergoop Unseen) is a better match.

Not recommended for:
- Monk tones 5-10 (visible white cast)
- Very dry skin without a moisturizer underneath (matte, slightly drying finish)
- Anyone wanting an invisible finish under makeup
