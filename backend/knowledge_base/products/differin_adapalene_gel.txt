Why it works:
Adapalene is a third-generation retinoid and the only prescription-strength retinoid sold over the counter in the US. It binds selectively to retinoic acid receptors in skin, normalizing how skin cells shed inside the pore so they stop forming microcomedones (the precursor of every pimple). It also has anti-inflammatory effects. Faster cell turnover gradually fades post-acne marks. Results take 8-12 weeks, and the first 2-4 weeks often bring dryness, peeling, and a temporary "purge".

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 1-6: Strong long-term acne prevention. Start every other night.
- Tones 7-10: Adapalene helps fade PIH over time, but the retinization phase (redness, peeling) can itself create new dark patches on deeper skin. Start 2-3 nights per week, use the "sandwich" method (moisturizer, adapalene, moisturizer), and never combine with other exfoliants at the start.
- ALL tones: Increases sun sensitivity; daily SPF is essential.

Not recommended for:
- Pregnancy or trying to conceive (retinoid)
- Very sensitive or eczema-prone skin
- Layering with other retinoids, BHA/AHA, or benzoyl peroxide in the same night early on
- Monk tones 7-10 without a slow ramp-up and moisturizer buffering
