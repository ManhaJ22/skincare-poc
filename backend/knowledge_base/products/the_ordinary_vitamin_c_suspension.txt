Why it works:
Ascorbyl glucoside is a stable vitamin C derivative (vitamin C bonded to glucose). Unlike L-ascorbic acid, it does not oxidize in water, giving it a much longer shelf life and causing far less irritation. On skin, enzymes cleave the glucose bond, releasing free L-ascorbic acid gradually. At 12%, this delivers meaningful vitamin C activity for: inhibiting tyrosinase (the enzyme that produces melanin), neutralizing free radicals that cause UV damage, and supporting collagen synthesis. The derivative form is particularly appropriate for sensitive skin or anyone who has found pure vitamin C (L-ascorbic acid) irritating. Clinical studies show comparable brightening efficacy to lower concentrations of L-ascorbic acid over longer time frames (8+ weeks vs 4 weeks).

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 5-10: Ascorbyl glucoside is one of the preferred vitamin C forms for darker skin tones because it is gentle enough to use without risk of irritation-driven PIH. Pure L-ascorbic acid at 15-20% can cause stinging that becomes inflammation, which then triggers PIH — the opposite of the intended effect. This stabilized form avoids that risk while still delivering brightening and antioxidant benefit.
- Tones 1-4: Also effective. May prefer pure L-ascorbic acid for faster visible results (4-6 weeks vs 8-12 weeks), but this formula works and is more stable.
- ALL tones: Pairs excellently with niacinamide (both inhibit melanin transfer via different pathways, synergistic effect) and with SPF.

Not recommended for:
- Users wanting the fastest visible results (pure L-ascorbic acid serums work faster)
- Oily skin in humid climates (may feel slightly sticky — layer under a water-gel moisturizer)

Dermatologist note: Safe during pregnancy. No photosensitivity (unlike retinol). Store in a cool, dark place. Once opened, use within 6 months. No refrigeration required for this derivative form.
