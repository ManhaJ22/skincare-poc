Why it works:
Snail secretion filtrate (SSF) is a complex biological fluid containing: glycoproteins, hyaluronic acid, glycolic acid (AHA at mild natural concentration), allantoin, and growth factors (EGF-like peptides). The combination provides: (1) mild AHA exfoliation for cell turnover without the irritation of synthetic AHAs; (2) intense humectant hydration; (3) allantoin's keratolytic and soothing properties; (4) growth factor signals that support tissue repair. At 96%, this is among the most concentrated SSF products available. Clinical studies on SSF show wound healing acceleration, improved scar appearance, and reduction in PIH over 8+ weeks. The watery essence texture absorbs quickly and layers under all skin types' routines easily.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 5-10: Snail essence has an outstanding reputation in the K-beauty community specifically for reducing dark marks and PIH — skin concerns that disproportionately affect medium-to-deep skin tones. The growth factor and mild AHA combination accelerates the natural healing of post-inflammatory marks. Many users on tones 7-10 report visible PIH reduction within 4-6 weeks of twice-daily use.
- Tones 1-4: Excellent for post-acne red marks (erythema), barrier repair, and maintaining skin hydration. Also useful after procedures that disrupt the skin barrier.
- ALL tones: Supports barrier recovery after over-exfoliation, retinol introduction, or any procedure.
