PRODUCT: Neutrogena Hydro Boost Water Gel Face Moisturizer (1.7 oz, Signature Fragrance)
BRAND: Neutrogena
AMAZON ASIN: B00NR1YQHM
PRICE RANGE: $17-$26 (1.7 oz jar; about 2 months of twice-daily use)
ROUTINE STEP: moisturizer
TIME OF USE: both (morning and evening)

KEY INGREDIENTS:
- Hyaluronic acid (sodium hyaluronate): humectant that pulls water into the upper skin layers for a plumped, hydrated feel without oil.
- Glycerin: humectant that also supports barrier function.
- Dimethicone: silicone that forms a light, breathable film to reduce water loss and give a smooth finish.
- Olive extract: emollient plant extract (small amount).

SKIN TYPES: normal, oily, combination, dehydrated-but-oily. The gel texture absorbs fast and leaves no greasy film.

MONK SKIN TONES: works for all tones 1-10. Notes for deeper tones (Monk 6-10): no cast or residue. Light hydration helps prevent the dull, ashy look of dehydrated deeper skin without adding shine.

FRAGRANCE FREE: NO. This version contains added fragrance ("Signature Fragrance"). A fragrance-free version is sold separately. Users who want fragrance-free products, or who have fragrance allergies or sensitive skin, should not use this ASIN.
CRUELTY FREE: not certified; check the brand's current status if this matters to the user.
OIL FREE / NON-COMEDOGENIC: yes

WHY IT WORKS: Oily skin can still lack water (dehydration). Humectants such as hyaluronic acid and glycerin add water without lipids that would feel heavy on oily skin, and dimethicone slows evaporation. A hydrated barrier also tolerates actives (niacinamide, BHA, retinol) better.

NOT GOOD FOR:
- Very dry, flaky, or mature dry skin. It does not have enough occlusives or lipids, and a ceramide cream works better.
- Fragrance-sensitive users or those with a fragrance allergy (this version contains fragrance).
- Eczema-prone or compromised barriers.
