Why it works:
Bentonite clay carries a strong negative charge and swells when wet, which lets it absorb excess oil and pull debris from the skin surface and pore openings. That gives an immediate matte, "deep cleaned" feeling and can make pores look smaller for a day or two. It is a single, unscented ingredient. Mixing it with water (rather than apple cider vinegar, as the brand suggests) is gentler on the skin barrier.

Monk tone specific notes:
- ALL tones: The clay looks grey while on the face but rinses off completely; it does not leave a cast.
- Tones 6-10: It can be very drying. Overdrying leaves deeper skin looking ashy and can irritate, which risks new dark marks. Keep it to 5-10 minutes, once a week, and follow with moisturizer.

Not recommended for:
- Dry, sensitive, or eczema-prone skin
- Use more than once or twice a week
- Mixing with apple cider vinegar on reactive skin (stings)
- Leaving on until it cracks and pulls (over-drying)
