Why it works:
A lightweight, water-light serum built around calming hydration rather than strong actives. Hyaluronic acid in multiple molecular weights draws water into different depths of the outer skin layer, and green tea contributes antioxidant polyphenols (such as EGCG) with soothing, anti-redness properties. Panthenol (provitamin B5) is a humectant that also supports barrier repair, which is why it is a staple in products for reactive skin. The formula is non-comedogenic and absorbs quickly with a non-sticky finish, so it layers easily under moisturizer or alongside treatments.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 1-5: Redness and flushing show most visibly on lighter skin, and this serum's calming, barrier-supporting ingredients target exactly that.
- Tones 6-10: Useful as a soothing, hydrating layer when using actives like retinoids or acids. Keeping skin calm and hydrated reduces irritation, which lowers the risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH). Hydration also prevents the dull, ashy look dehydrated deeper skin can have.

Not recommended for:
- Users wanting a single serum that targets dark spots or acne (it is calming and hydrating, not an active treatment)
- Anyone with a known green tea or panthenol sensitivity
- Users who need a confirmed fragrance-free product (check the current label first)

Note: Patch test before full-face use, as with any new product.
