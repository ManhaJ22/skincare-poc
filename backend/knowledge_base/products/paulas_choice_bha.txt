PRODUCT: Paula's Choice Skin Perfecting 2% BHA Liquid Exfoliant (4 fl oz)
BRAND: Paula's Choice
AMAZON ASIN: B00949CTQQ
PRICE RANGE: $25-$37 (4 fl oz / 118 mL; about 3-4 months at once-daily use)
ROUTINE STEP: treatment
TIME OF USE: evening preferred (start 2-3 nights per week, increase to daily if tolerated). If used in the morning, follow with SPF.

KEY INGREDIENTS:
- Salicylic acid 2% (BHA): oil-soluble exfoliant that gets into pores, dissolves the sebum and dead-cell plugs that form blackheads and clogged pores, and is anti-inflammatory.
- Green tea extract (Camellia sinensis): antioxidant with soothing properties that offsets some of the acid's irritation.
- Methylpropanediol: solvent that helps salicylic acid penetrate and adds light hydration.
- Formulated at pH ~3.2-3.8, which keeps the salicylic acid effective.

SKIN TYPES: oily, combination, acne-prone, normal with clogged pores or rough texture.

MONK SKIN TONES: works for all tones 1-10. Notes for deeper tones (Monk 6-10): salicylic acid is generally well tolerated on deeper skin and helps fade post-acne marks by speeding cell turnover. However, over-exfoliation causes irritation, and irritation can trigger post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) on deeper skin. Start slowly (2-3 times per week) and stop if there is stinging, peeling, or new darkening.

FRAGRANCE FREE: yes
CRUELTY FREE: yes (Leaping Bunny certified)
NON-COMEDOGENIC: yes

WHY IT WORKS: Water-soluble AHAs work on the surface. Salicylic acid is lipophilic, so it penetrates oil-filled pores, which makes it the best-studied OTC exfoliant for blackheads, whiteheads, and texture. It is also chemically related to aspirin and calms inflamed blemishes.

NOT GOOD FOR:
- People with aspirin/salicylate allergy. Avoid this product.
- Pregnancy (leave-on salicylic acid). Ask a healthcare provider.
- Very dry, sensitive, or eczema-prone skin, or a damaged barrier.
- Using the same night as retinol or other strong exfoliants. Alternate nights.
- Budgets under ~$25/month total. It is a larger upfront cost, though it lasts several months.
