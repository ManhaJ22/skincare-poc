Why it works:
Azelaic acid is a dicarboxylic acid naturally found in wheat, rye, and barley. Its mechanism is multi-functional: (1) inhibits tyrosinase — reducing melanin production and hyperpigmentation; (2) keratolytic effect — mild exfoliation that normalizes cell turnover without the irritation risk of AHAs or BHAs; (3) anti-inflammatory — reduces redness, papules, and pustules associated with rosacea and acne; (4) mildly antibacterial against C. acnes. Prescription azelaic acid is available at 15-20% — at 10% OTC, this provides gentler but still clinically meaningful benefits. The silicone base (dimethicone, isododecane) creates a smooth application that minimizes the gritty texture some azelaic acid formulas have. This is one of the safest active ingredients available.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 4-10: Azelaic acid is arguably the BEST PIH treatment for the OTC space for medium-to-deep skin tones. Unlike BHAs and retinol, it is extremely unlikely to cause irritation that worsens PIH. It specifically targets the melanin overproduction at the site of inflammation, making it ideal for post-acne dark marks.
- Tones 7-10: Safe to use daily. Pairs exceptionally well with niacinamide serum (complementary mechanisms for pigmentation) and vitamin C. This combination (azelaic acid + niacinamide + SPF) is considered the cornerstone PIH-fighting regimen for deeper skin tones.
- Tones 1-3: Excellent for rosacea — 10% OTC azelaic acid has clinical evidence for reducing rosacea papules and redness comparable to some prescription treatments.

Not recommended for:
- Known azelaic acid allergy (very rare)
- Users expecting the same speed as retinoids (this is gentler and slower — 8-12+ weeks for visible change)

Dermatologist note: SAFE during pregnancy — one of the only proven hyperpigmentation treatments considered safe for pregnant users. No photosensitivity. Can be combined with most other actives. FDA-approved for rosacea at 15% (prescription). This OTC 10% formula is an excellent starting point.
