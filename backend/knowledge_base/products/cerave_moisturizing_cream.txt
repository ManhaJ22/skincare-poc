Why it works:
A rich, occlusive moisturizer with a ceramide-dominant formula that genuinely repairs the skin barrier rather than just masking dryness. The combination of ceramides 1, 3, and 6-II mimics the natural lipid matrix of the stratum corneum. Petrolatum (2.5%) is one of the most effective occlusives known — it reduces TEWL by up to 99% when applied in a thick layer. Hyaluronic acid provides humectant hydration. MVE (MultiVesicular Emulsion) technology releases ingredients slowly over 24 hours. This cream is thicker than the lotion version, making it more appropriate for very dry or eczema-prone skin.

Monk tone specific notes:
- Tones 7-10: Excellent for post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation maintenance — a well-hydrated skin barrier heals dark marks faster. The petrolatum in this formula can feel heavy on naturally oilier skin; test the lotion version first.
- Tones 1-3: Ideal for rosacea-prone skin and eczema. The barrier repair properties help reduce sensitivity triggers.
- ALL tones: Petrolatum creates a slight sheen — may not be preferred for daytime use in humid climates or by those with oily skin.

Not recommended for:
- Oily or acne-prone skin (petrolatum can cause milia — tiny white bumps — in some users)
- Hot or humid climates for daytime use
- Those who dislike a heavier texture

Dermatologist note: Safe during pregnancy and breastfeeding. Recommended by the National Eczema Association. Non-comedogenic claims are disputed for some users — particularly on forehead and chin. Safe for use on body as well as face.
